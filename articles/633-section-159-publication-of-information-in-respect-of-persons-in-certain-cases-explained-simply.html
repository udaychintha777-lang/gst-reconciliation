Section 159: Publication of information in respect of persons in certain cases — Explained Simply

Section 159 deals with publication of information in respect of persons in certain cases. The provision should be read with the connected rules, notifications and the facts of the transaction.

What this means: The statutory subject is publication of information in respect of persons in certain cases. In practice, the section works with definitions, rules, forms and tax-period-specific changes. A business should therefore check the complete compliance chain rather than relying on the section title alone.

Key points

  • Understand the transaction before applying the tax treatment.
  • Check the applicable section, rule and tax-period-specific changes.
  • Match the accounting entry with the GST return and supporting documents.
  • Document the judgement where the treatment depends on facts or contractual terms.

Example: A business identifies the transaction, checks the applicable treatment, records the tax correctly, reconciles it with the return and retains the supporting documents.
