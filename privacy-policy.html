Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit GSTReconciliation.in, use its tools or contact the website.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information and message you choose to send, such as your email address and the contents of your request.

Website analytics and advertising

The website may use analytics, hosting, security and advertising services, including Google services, to understand site usage, maintain the website and, if approved, display advertisements. These providers may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses or similar technologies in accordance with their own policies and your applicable choices and settings.

Google explains its data practices and advertising controls in its own privacy and advertising documentation. Visitors should review Google's policies and use available consent or ad-personalization controls where applicable.

Files submitted to browser tools

Some tools may allow you to select or process spreadsheets or other files. Before uploading sensitive information, review the specific tool's instructions. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, bank credentials or information you are not authorized to share. Unless a tool expressly states otherwise, do not assume that files are transmitted, stored, or deleted in a particular way without reviewing its current implementation and behavior.

Cookies and choices

Your browser can provide controls to block or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies may affect website functionality or advertising preferences.

Third-party services and links

The website may link to third-party websites or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices, not this policy.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to operating a small informational website, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was provided, operational needs and applicable requirements.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the website and its services change. Material updates will be reflected on this page. For privacy questions, contact uday@gstreconciliation.in.
