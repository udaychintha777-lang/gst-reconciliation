Important decision point

GSTR-2B is a static, auto-drafted ITC statement. Reconciliation is a control over differences; it does not by itself establish that every credit is legally eligible. Final ITC decisions should consider the GST law, invoice/supporting documents, reversals and other applicable conditions.

Read the result correctly

Use the categories as a structured review queue rather than treating every exception as a tax error.

Matched

The compared records satisfy the configured matching logic. This is the starting point for routine review, not a substitute for ITC eligibility testing.

Partial / Review Required

A related record exists but one or more fields differ. Check invoice number formatting, dates, taxable value, tax amounts, document type and amendments.

Not in 2B / Not in PR

One source has a record that the other source does not. Investigate supplier filing timing, missed books entries, duplicates, amendments or source-data issues.

GST RECONCILIATION KNOWLEDGE

GST Reconciliation Tool — Purchase Register vs GSTR-2B

Use this GST ITC reconciliation tool to compare your Purchase Register with the GSTR-2B data downloaded from the GST portal. The objective is not simply to mark invoices as matched or unmatched; it is to identify the documents and tax differences that require accounting, supplier or GST compliance review before ITC is relied upon.

What is GST reconciliation?

GST reconciliation is the process of comparing purchase and input-tax records maintained in the books with the tax information appearing in GSTR-2B. For each document, the reviewer checks supplier GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date, taxable value and GST amounts, then determines whether the record can be treated as matched or requires further investigation.

GSTR-2B is a static, auto-drafted ITC statement. GSTN advises taxpayers to reconcile GSTR-2B with their own records and books, avoid duplicate ITC, make required reversals and ensure applicable reverse-charge tax is paid. This makes a structured purchase-register-to-GSTR-2B reconciliation an important control in monthly GST working.

This page is designed for CAs, accountants, tax teams, finance managers, internal auditors and businesses that need an invoice-level GST ITC matching workpaper rather than only a headline total.

04 · Review

Prioritise tax differences and exceptions, investigate suppliers and retain the Excel workpaper for the GST review file.

Why invoice-level reconciliation matters

A difference in the monthly ITC total does not tell the reviewer which supplier or invoice caused the difference. Invoice-level reconciliation converts that total into an actionable review list. A purchase invoice may exist in the books but not yet appear in GSTR-2B; a supplier may have reported a different taxable value or tax amount; an invoice may be duplicated in the purchase data; or a credit/debit note may require separate consideration.

The reconciliation result should therefore be used as a working control, not as an automatic legal conclusion that every unmatched invoice is permanently ineligible. Eligibility also depends on the GST Act, Rules, nature of supply, documentation, timing, reversals and other applicable conditions.

Frequently asked questions about GST reconciliation

What is a GST reconciliation tool?

A GST reconciliation tool compares purchase or input-tax records with GST portal data such as GSTR-2B and produces document-level matching and exception results for review.

What does GSTR-2B reconciliation mean?

It means comparing the GSTR-2B documents and tax amounts with the taxpayer's own purchase records to identify matched documents, differences and missing records before ITC reporting decisions are finalised.

Can I reconcile GSTR-2B Excel with a Purchase Register?

Yes. This tool is designed around an Excel Purchase Register and the GSTR-2B workbook downloaded from the GST portal, followed by invoice-level matching and exception review.

What should I do when an invoice is in the Purchase Register but not in GSTR-2B?

Treat it as an exception for investigation. Check supplier filing, GSTIN, invoice details, period differences and other applicable GST conditions before deciding the ITC treatment.

What is a tax amount mismatch?

It is a situation where a document can be related between the two datasets but the taxable value or GST amount differs. The difference should be reviewed against the invoice, supplier filing and accounting entry.

Why is supplier GSTIN important in reconciliation?

Supplier GSTIN is a key identifier that helps distinguish documents from different suppliers and reduces false matches where invoice numbers are similar.

Is this GST reconciliation tool free and do I need to sign up?

Yes. The tool is 100% free to use, no sign-up is required, and you can run reconciliations as many times as needed.
