Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017

Rule 3 — Intimation for composition levy

A practical guide to the procedural intimations connected with opting for composition levy under section 10 of the CGST Act.

Rule 3 Chapter II — Composition Levy Current provision

What does Rule 3 provide?

Rule 3 sets out the principal procedural intimations and related stock-information requirements for persons opting to pay tax under the composition levy provisions of section 10.

In simple terms: the Rule tells an eligible person which GST form is used at the relevant stage to communicate the composition option, and it also prescribes related information that must be furnished in specified circumstances.

Key compliance requirements

1. Provisional-registration route

A person covered by the provisional-registration provisions who opts for section 10 is required to electronically intimate the option in FORM GST CMP-01, subject to the timing and other conditions prescribed in the Rule. The Rule also contains a consequence for late filing after the appointed day: tax is not to be collected from the appointed day and a bill of supply is to be issued for supplies made after that day.

2. Option during registration application

A person applying for registration under Rule 8 may indicate the option to pay tax under section 10 in Part B of FORM GST REG-01. The Rule treats that declaration as the relevant intimation for composition levy.

3. Existing registered person opting for composition

An existing registered person opting for section 10 is required to electronically furnish FORM GST CMP-02 within the prescribed time before the financial year for which the option is exercised. The Rule also links the procedure with the input-tax-credit reversal statement in FORM GST ITC-03 under Rule 44.

4. Special historical transition provisions

Rule 3 contains sub-rule (3A), which dealt with a specified transitional route for persons opting for composition during the initial GST implementation period. Because this provision concerns a historical transition, its dates should not be treated as a general current-year deadline.

5. Stock details

For the route covered by sub-rule (1), the person is required to furnish prescribed stock details, including inward supplies of goods received from unregistered persons, through FORM GST CMP-03 within the period specified in the Rule, subject to any extension permitted under the Rules.

6. PAN-level effect

The Rule provides that an intimation under the specified composition provisions for a place of business in a State or Union territory is treated as an intimation for the other places of business registered on the same Permanent Account Number.

Forms connected with Rule 3

  • FORM GST CMP-01 — composition option for the specified provisional-registration route.
  • FORM GST REG-01 — composition option can be indicated in Part B during the registration application route described in Rule 3.
  • FORM GST CMP-02 — intimation by a registered person opting for composition levy under section 10.
  • FORM GST ITC-03 — statement connected with reversal/payment of input tax credit as required under Rule 44 in the circumstances covered by Rule 3.
  • FORM GST CMP-03 — stock details for the route covered by sub-rule (1).

Practical significance

  • Composition taxpayers should identify the correct entry route before filing the relevant form.
  • The filing date matters because Rule 3 prescribes different procedural timings for different situations.
  • The option under section 10 should be read together with the eligibility conditions and restrictions in the CGST Act and subsequent Rules.
  • Rule 4 determines the effective date of the composition option in specified situations, so Rule 3 should not be read in isolation.
  • For current compliance, taxpayers should verify the latest portal procedure, applicable forms and any amendments or extensions notified for the relevant period.
