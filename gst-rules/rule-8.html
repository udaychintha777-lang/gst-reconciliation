Rule 8 – Application for Registration

At a glance

What Rule 8 does

Rule 8 lays down the procedure for making an application for GST registration. The application is made electronically in FORM GST REG-01 through the prescribed portal process, subject to the applicant category and applicable verification requirements.

Simple way to understand Rule 8: Rule 8 is the application stage. Rule 9 deals with verification and grant of registration, while Rule 10 deals with the certificate and GSTIN communication process.

The current Rule 8 framework includes preliminary details, PAN-linked validation, Part A and Part B of FORM GST REG-01, document submission, verification steps and the application-completion process. Later amendments have also introduced risk-based biometric Aadhaar authentication and document verification for identified applicants.

1. Rule 8 — statutory framework

Rule 8 prescribes the electronic application procedure for registration. The applicant uses FORM GST REG-01 and provides the required information and supporting documents in the prescribed manner.

The rule works together with the GST registration provisions rather than operating as a standalone eligibility test. The taxpayer must first determine whether registration is required or available under the Act and then complete the application process correctly.

Important: Filing REG-01 does not by itself mean that GST registration has been granted. The application is subject to verification and the subsequent registration process under the connected rules.

2. GST REG-01 application process in simple language

1Start Part A

Enter the preliminary details requested in the application, including the State/UT and PAN-linked business information.

2Validation and TRN

The system validates the preliminary information and provides a Temporary Reference Number where applicable, which can be used to continue the application.

3Complete Part B

Provide the detailed business, constitution, promoter/partner/director, principal place, additional places, bank and other required particulars.

4Upload supporting documents

Documents should match the legal name, constitution and business details provided in the application.

5Complete authentication / verification

Depending on the applicant and risk-based process, Aadhaar authentication or biometric/KYC verification may be required.

6Submit and obtain acknowledgement

Once the application is completed and submitted as prescribed, an acknowledgement/ARN is generated for tracking.

3. Part A of FORM GST REG-01

Part A is the preliminary stage of the registration application. It establishes the basic identity and jurisdictional information required before the detailed application can proceed.

Information areaPractical control
State / Union TerritorySelect the State/UT from which the relevant taxable supplies are made or registration is otherwise required.
Legal nameUse the legal name linked to the PAN records rather than an informal trade name.
PANEnter the correct PAN of the business or proprietor as applicable.
Email / mobileUse active contact details capable of receiving verification messages.
Common error: A mismatch between PAN records, legal name and the information entered in REG-01 can delay the registration process. Verify the source documents before starting the application.

4. Part B — detailed registration information

Part B is where the applicant provides the detailed information needed to establish the business and its GST registration profile.

Business constitution
Proprietorship, partnership, company, LLP, trust, society, government entity and other applicable categories.
Principal place of business
Address, possession/occupancy evidence and business activity details.
Additional places
Other registered places of business should be identified where a separate place is required to be disclosed.
Promoters / authorised persons
Details of partners, directors, promoters or authorised signatories as applicable.
Goods and services
Relevant goods/services and classification information should be entered accurately.
Bank details
Provide bank account information in accordance with the applicable registration requirements and portal process.

5. Documents — what businesses should prepare

Rule 8 should be approached as a document-matching exercise. The information entered in REG-01 should be capable of being supported by the underlying records.

Business type / issueTypical supporting evidence to prepare
ProprietorshipPAN/identity details and principal-place/business documents as applicable.
Partnership / LLPConstitution documents, PAN and partner/designated-partner information.
CompanyCertificate/constitutional records, PAN, director details and authorised-signatory information.
Place of businessOwnership, lease, rent, consent or other applicable possession documents.
Authorised signatoryAuthorisation and identity/KYC information as applicable.

The exact documents can vary by constitution and circumstances. The practical objective is consistency: legal name, PAN, constitution, address and authorised person should tell the same story across all documents.

6. Aadhaar authentication, biometric verification and KYC

The registration process can include Aadhaar authentication or other biometric/KYC verification depending on the applicant's circumstances and the applicable portal/risk-based process.

Rule 8 was amended to provide for biometric-based Aadhaar authentication and photograph/document verification for applicants identified through the prescribed process. The 2022 amendment specifically introduced the risk-based mechanism in Rule 8(4A). citeturn1search1

Practical point: Do not treat “application submitted” as the same as “application complete.” Where the prescribed authentication or facilitation-centre verification is applicable, the registration process may not be treated as complete until that step is finished.

7. TRN and ARN — understand the difference

ReferenceMeaningWhen useful
TRNTemporary Reference Number generated during the application process.Used to continue and access a registration application before final submission.
ARNApplication Reference Number generated after successful submission/acknowledgement.Used to track the submitted registration application.
GSTINGST registration identification number issued after registration is granted.Used as the taxpayer's GST registration identifier after approval.
Remember: TRN is not GSTIN. ARN is not GSTIN. A business should not represent itself as registered merely because it has received a TRN or ARN.

8. Multiple places of business and registrations

Rule 8 requires careful attention where a business has more than one place of business or operates through different establishments.

The application process should clearly distinguish the principal place from additional places and should use the correct registration structure applicable to the business.

Same State
Review whether additional places need to be disclosed under the same registration or whether separate registration is required/permitted under the Act.
Different State
GST registration is generally State/UT based. A business making taxable supplies from another State should separately analyse the registration requirement there.

For SEZ units and other special registration situations, the specific statutory provisions must be checked before filing REG-01.

9. Composition option in REG-01

The registration application can also contain the option/intimation to pay tax under Section 10 where the applicant is eligible and chooses the composition route.

This is important because the registration application is not only about obtaining a GSTIN. It can also establish the taxpayer's intended tax-payment route at registration.

Control: Before selecting composition in REG-01, complete the eligibility analysis under Section 10 and the relevant composition rules. Do not select the option merely because the expected turnover is below the threshold.

See Rule 3, Rule 4, Rule 5, Rule 6 and Rule 7 for the composition framework.

10. Practical examples

Example 1 — New private limited company

A newly incorporated company begins taxable business from a rented office. Before filing REG-01, the finance team should ensure the company PAN, incorporation/constitutional records, authorised signatory details and address documents are consistent. If the address document shows one entity name while the application shows another, clarification may be required.

Example 2 — Partnership firm changes its office

A partnership firm moves to a new office. The GST registration application/amendment information should not be prepared from an old registration document. The current possession/occupancy evidence and legal constitution should be reviewed before submission.

Example 3 — Business has three branches

A business has a head office and three additional places in the same State. The finance team should map each location and determine the correct registration structure before filing. The application should not simply list addresses without understanding whether each place is required to be registered or disclosed.

Example 4 — Applicant chooses composition

A small business expects to qualify for composition. Before selecting the composition option in REG-01, the business checks Section 10 eligibility, business activities, customer profile and composition restrictions. The option is selected only after the legal eligibility review.

11. Common GST registration application mistakes

  1. PAN/legal-name mismatch.
  2. Incorrect constitution of business.
  3. Weak or inconsistent principal-place documents.
  4. Incorrect authorised-signatory details.
  5. Using an inactive email/mobile number.
  6. Entering an incorrect place of business.
  7. Uploading documents that do not match the application.
  8. Ignoring the applicable Aadhaar/biometric/KYC step.
  9. Confusing TRN/ARN with GSTIN.
  10. Selecting composition without first checking Section 10.

12. Audit and departmental verification relevance

The registration application becomes an important historical record. During a future GST audit or departmental proceeding, the department may compare the registered particulars with the taxpayer's actual business operations.

AreaPossible verification question
Legal name / PANDoes the GST registration match the taxpayer's legal identity?
Business addressWas the business actually operating from the registered place?
Additional placesWere other places of business correctly reported?
Business activityDoes the actual activity match the registration particulars?
Composition optionWas the taxpayer eligible for the chosen composition route?
Authorised personWere filings and representations made by the appropriately authorised person?
Practical lesson: GST registration data should be treated as a controlled master record. If the business changes materially, the GST registration particulars should be reviewed for amendment rather than allowing the registration profile to become outdated.

14. Management checklist before filing REG-01

  • Confirm whether registration is mandatory, voluntary or required due to a specific statutory condition.
  • Confirm the correct State/UT registration.
  • Verify PAN and legal name.
  • Confirm constitution of business.
  • Verify principal-place documents.
  • Map all additional places of business.
  • Confirm authorised signatory and promoter/director/partner information.
  • Prepare supporting KYC documents.
  • Check whether Aadhaar/biometric verification may apply.
  • Decide whether the composition option is legally available before selecting it.
  • Review the completed REG-01 internally before final submission.
  • Retain TRN, ARN and submitted application records.

16. Frequently asked questions

What is GST Rule 8?

Rule 8 of the CGST Rules prescribes the procedure for applying for GST registration, principally through FORM GST REG-01 and the prescribed electronic verification process.

What is FORM GST REG-01?

REG-01 is the prescribed application form used for GST registration for the applicant categories covered by the form and registration rules.

What is TRN in GST registration?

TRN means Temporary Reference Number. It is generated during the registration application process and can be used to continue the application before final submission.

What is ARN in GST registration?

ARN means Application Reference Number. It is associated with the submitted registration application and is used for tracking the application.

Is TRN the same as GSTIN?

No. TRN is a temporary application reference, while GSTIN is the registration identification number issued after registration is granted.

Is Aadhaar authentication required for every GST applicant?

The applicable authentication process depends on the applicant and the prescribed rules, exemptions and risk-based identification. Some applicants may be required to complete biometric/KYC verification.

Can composition be selected while applying for GST registration?

Where eligible, the registration application provides for the composition option/intimation. The applicant should complete the Section 10 eligibility analysis before selecting it.

Why is GST registration application accuracy important?

The registration profile becomes the taxpayer's official GST master data. Incorrect legal name, address, constitution, authorised person or business activity can create future amendment, return, invoice and audit issues.

17. Practical takeaway

Rule 8 is best understood as the controlled onboarding process for a GST registration. The objective is not simply to obtain a GSTIN quickly; it is to create a registration profile that accurately represents the legal entity and its actual business.

Best sequence: Determine registration requirement → identify State/UT → verify PAN/legal identity → prepare documents → complete REG-01 → complete authentication/KYC → obtain ARN → track application → configure ERP only after the registration particulars are confirmed.

A careful REG-01 application reduces downstream problems involving invoices, returns, amendments, branch registrations, composition status and departmental verification.

18. Source & verification

Primary legal basis: Rule 8 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, read with the registration provisions of the CGST Act and connected registration rules.

CBIC material identifies FORM GST REG-01 as the application for registration and sets out the Part A/Part B process. Amendments to Rule 8 have also introduced specific Aadhaar/biometric and risk-based verification mechanisms.

This page is a practical educational explanation. Registration procedures, portal workflows, documents and verification requirements can be amended. For a live application, use the requirements applicable on the date of filing.
