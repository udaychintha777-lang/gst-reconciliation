Source & Legal Notice

This library uses the individually identifiable Rule entries in the current India Code 2.0 record for the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017. The source collection currently reports 214 Rules while 213 individual Rule entries are listed on the page; this library does not invent an additional unverified Rule. Some listed entries are marked omitted/historical and are displayed with that status. Always verify the applicable Act, Rules, notifications, circulars and Gazette publications for the relevant tax period before relying on a provision.
