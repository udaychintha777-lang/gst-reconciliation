About GSTReconciliation.in

GSTReconciliation.in is an independent educational website focused on Goods and Services Tax (GST) knowledge, practical compliance workflows and browser-based tools for accounting and tax professionals, business owners and learners.

What the website provides

  • Educational articles explaining GST concepts, return filing, input tax credit, reconciliation and compliance workflows.
  • Practical GST tools, including Purchase Register and GSTR-2B reconciliation utilities.
  • Reference resources intended to help users organize their own compliance review and accounting work.

Editorial approach

Content is prepared for general educational use and may include illustrative examples. GST law, rules, notifications, circulars, portal functionality and judicial decisions can change. Readers should check primary government sources and consider their own facts before relying on information for a filing, payment or legal position.
