Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GSTReconciliation.in handles information when visitors browse the website, use its browser-based tools or contact us. The website provides GST education, reference material and calculation or spreadsheet utilities. It does not currently require visitors to create an account to use the public pages and tools.

1. Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, name (if supplied), message and any attachments you choose to send. Please do not email passwords, portal credentials, access tokens, bank credentials, Aadhaar/PAN details or confidential taxpayer files unless they are strictly necessary and you are authorised to share them.

2. Files processed by browser-based tools

Some tools let you select Excel or CSV files, including the GST reconciliation, duplicate invoice, GSTR-2B analysis, GSTR-3B ITC working, ITC mismatch and purchase-register analysis utilities. In the current source code reviewed for these tools, the selected spreadsheet is read and processed in your browser using JavaScript and spreadsheet libraries. We did not find code that uploads the workbook contents to a GSTReconciliation server for processing.

The reconciliation tool may send limited tool-usage events to Google Analytics, such as the selected file type and number of sheets. The inspected event code does not send the spreadsheet rows or workbook contents. Your browser may also request third-party library files from content-delivery networks to make the tools work. Those third parties receive the technical information normally associated with a browser request.

This description reflects the current implementation inspected on the effective date. Tool behavior may change when the website is updated. Each tool's on-screen instructions should be read before use. Do not use a shared or public computer for confidential files, and close the tool when you have finished. You remain responsible for deciding whether you are authorised to process the data you select.

3. Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 on certain pages to understand traffic and improve the website. Depending on the page and settings, Google Analytics may process information such as page views, interactions, browser and device information, approximate location, cookie or other identifiers and technical network information. Google explains that IP addresses are used at collection to derive location information and are discarded before Google Analytics data is logged or stored. Some pages also configure the Google tag with an IP-anonymisation setting.

We do not intentionally send spreadsheet contents, taxpayer records or other file data to Google Analytics. Visitors can manage cookies through browser settings. Learn more at Google Analytics data collection and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Advertising and advertising cookies

Google AdSense code is present on certain website pages. If advertising is served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, limit frequency of and report on advertisements. Depending on the visitor's settings, applicable law and the advertising configuration, ads may be contextual or personalised. The website's advertising status and ad configuration may change.

Visitors can review or change Google's advertising personalisation settings at Google Ads Settings. Information about AdSense cookies is available in How AdSense uses cookies. Visitors may also review the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics and advertising services may use first-party or third-party cookies and similar technologies. These technologies help distinguish browsers, measure site use, support advertising and prevent repeated or unsuitable ad delivery. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some website features or advertising preferences may not work as intended if cookies are blocked.

6. Third-party services

The website uses or may use third-party services such as Google Analytics, Google advertising services and content-delivery networks that provide JavaScript libraries. When your browser connects to those services, the providers may process technical request information under their own privacy terms. This website does not control the independent privacy practices of those providers.

7. Retention and security

We do not operate a visitor account system for the public tools described above. Emails and correspondence are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain relevant records and meet applicable requirements. Browser-based tool files are processed in the browser by the inspected code; we do not maintain a website-side file-upload repository for those tools in the implementation reviewed. No website or internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit information or select files. You can manage cookies through your browser and advertising preferences through the controls linked above. If you have a privacy question or request, contact uday@gstreconciliation.in.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the website, tools, third-party services or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
