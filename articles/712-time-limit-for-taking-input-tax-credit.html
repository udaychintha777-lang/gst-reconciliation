Time limit for taking input tax credit

The time limit for taking input tax credit is governed by section 16(4), subject to the specific amendments and transitional or special provisions that may apply to a transaction or financial year. The applicable deadline must therefore be checked for the relevant tax period rather than relying on an old rule remembered from an earlier year.

What this means: A practical ITC control should track invoice date, financial year, annual return status, amendments/debit notes and the statutory deadline applicable to the invoice. Where later amendments changed the time limit or created special treatment, the specific provision and notification should be checked.

Key points

  • Understand the transaction before applying the tax treatment.
  • Check the applicable section, rule and tax-period-specific changes.
  • Match the accounting entry with the GST return and supporting documents.
  • Document the judgement where the treatment depends on facts or contractual terms.

Practical example

Example: A finance team maintains an invoice-ageing report and flags invoices approaching the statutory ITC deadline so that eligible credit is not lost through an avoidable compliance lapse.

Practical checklist

  • Identify the transaction and tax period
  • Check the applicable law and connected rules
  • Verify invoices, agreements, ledgers and portal data
  • Reconcile the working with the relevant return
  • Retain the final working and supporting evidence
