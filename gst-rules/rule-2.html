Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017

Rule 2 — Definitions

A practical guide to the defined expressions used within the CGST Rules, 2017 and the way undefined terms are generally traced back to the CGST Act.

Rule 2 Chapter I — Preliminary Current provision

What does Rule 2 provide?

Rule 2 contains the definitions that apply specifically for the purposes of the CGST Rules, unless the context requires otherwise.

In simple terms: Rule 2 explains a small set of expressions used in the Rules. Where a word or expression is not defined in the Rules but is defined in the CGST Act, its meaning is generally taken from the Act.

Key definitions

1. “Act”

For the Rules, “Act” refers to the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

2. “Form”

“Form” refers to a Form appended to the CGST Rules. This is important because many procedural requirements under the Rules are implemented through prescribed GST forms.

3. “Section”

“Section” means a section of the Act. This connects references made in the Rules to the corresponding provisions of the CGST Act.

4. “Special Economic Zone”

The expression has the meaning assigned to it in section 2(za) of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005.

5. Undefined expressions

Where an expression is used in the Rules but is not defined there, and the expression is defined in the CGST Act, the meaning assigned by the Act applies unless the context requires otherwise.

Practical significance

  • Rule 2 provides the starting point for interpreting the specific expressions it defines.
  • References to “Act”, “Form” and “section” in later Rules should be read in light of Rule 2.
  • For terms not defined in the Rules, the CGST Act may provide the applicable statutory meaning.
  • Cross-referenced legislation can also become relevant where Rule 2 expressly adopts a meaning from another law.
  • For legal or compliance decisions, the applicable version of the Rules and the relevant amendment notifications should be checked for the relevant tax period.
