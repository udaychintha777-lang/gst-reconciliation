GSTR-3B Interest Calculator

Select the tax period and State, enter only the tax actually paid through the Electronic Cash Ledger under IGST, CGST and SGST/UTGST, and enter the actual filing date. The calculator determines the applicable return due date from the filing pattern and applies recorded due-date extensions in its rule set.

Legal basis: Section 50(1) of the CGST Act provides interest for delayed payment of tax; section 50(2) provides that interest is calculated from the day succeeding the day on which tax was due. Rule 88B prescribes the manner of calculation, including the net tax liability/cash-ledger treatment for delayed filing situations.

Official basis checked against GST/CBIC material: Section 50 of the CGST Act; Rule 88B of the CGST Rules; GSTN's January 2026 advisory on enhanced GSTR-3B interest computation; GSTN QRMP due-date guidance. This calculator is an estimate and should be reconciled with the GST Portal's system-calculated interest before payment.

How the due date is determined

For monthly filers, the normal GSTR-3B due date is the 20th of the succeeding month, subject to government extensions. For QRMP quarterly filers, the GST Portal's guidance provides the 22nd/24th state-group due-date structure. The calculator uses the State only when the selected filing frequency is quarterly.

Government notifications can change a due date or provide special relief for a specified class, tax period or State. The calculator therefore contains an internal extension/relaxation rule table and displays the rule applied in the result instead of asking the user to manually enter the due date.
