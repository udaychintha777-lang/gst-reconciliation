# Google AdSense setup

The website contains responsive advertisement spaces and is ready for AdSense integration. Actual ads cannot be activated until Google approves the site and provides the publisher ID.

After approval, use the exact AdSense code supplied in your AdSense account and place it in the `<head>` of the pages where Google instructs you to place it. Do not invent a publisher ID.

Google currently requires the site to be added to AdSense, connected/verified and reviewed before ads can serve.
