Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017

Rule 1 — Short title and commencement

A plain-language guide to the opening provision of the CGST Rules, covering the name of the Rules and the date from which they came into force.

Rule 1 Preliminary Active / foundational provision

What does Rule 1 provide?

Rule 1 is the introductory provision of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017. It establishes the formal name of the subordinate legislation and specifies its commencement.

In simple terms: the Rules are formally known as the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, and the Rules came into force on 22 June 2017.

Detailed explanation

1. Short title

The provision gives the Rules their official short title. This is the name used when referring to the rules made under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

2. Commencement

The provision states the commencement date as 22 June 2017. This identifies the point from which the CGST Rules, 2017 came into operation, subject to later amendments and provisions that may have their own specified effective dates.

3. Why this matters

Rule 1 is mainly a foundational provision rather than a day-to-day compliance rule. Its importance is in establishing the identity and original commencement of the Rules. When researching an amended provision, the relevant amendment notification and its effective date should also be checked rather than relying only on the original commencement date.

Practical significance for taxpayers

  • It identifies the subordinate legislation as the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017.
  • It records 22 June 2017 as the commencement date of the Rules.
  • For historical analysis, the original commencement date should be distinguished from later amendment effective dates.
  • For a current compliance position, the latest amended version of the relevant Rule and the notification bringing an amendment into force should be verified.
